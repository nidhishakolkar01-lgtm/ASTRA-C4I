### 🎙️ Verbal Narration Script

#### [00:00 - 00:35] - Part 1: The Hook & The Problem
> **Presenter (Voiceover):**  
> *"Modern conflicts have proven that low-cost drones, employed individually and in swarms, have become a decisive battlefield factor. Yet today, training our troops still relies on classroom lectures and rare, weather-dependent live-fire drills that cost lakhs of rupees per repetition.*  
> *Under Problem Statement 26247 by the Ministry of Defence and Defence Services Staff College, we present **ASTRA-CUAS**: an indigenous, unit-deployable Counter-Drone Threat Simulation Trainer that runs smoothly on standard field laptops without requiring expensive GPU rigs—while remaining fully VR-capable."*

#### [00:35 - 01:20] - Part 2: Multi-Spectral Sensor Simulation
> **Presenter:**  
> *"Our platform doesn't just show a 3D drone—it simulates the authentic multi-spectral sensor suite used by our armed forces.*  
> *On the right, you see our 3D PPI Search Radar sweeping at 12 RPM, tracking radar cross-sections down to 0.01 square meters.*  
> *Below it, our real-time RF Spectrum Waterfall detects frequency-hopping control links across 2.4 and 5.8 GHz.*  
> *And with a single keystroke, the operator can switch from daylight optics to Gen-3 Night Vision or FLIR Thermal white-hot and ironbow modes to pierce through battlefield smoke, sandstorms, and fog."*

#### [01:20 - 02:05] - Part 3: Layered Arsenal & Dual-Terminal Red Team
> **Presenter:**  
> *"When a threat is classified, ASTRA-CUAS provides a layered counter-drone arsenal. In urban sectors with civilian presence, operators deploy directional RF disruptors, GNSS coordinate spoofers, or pinpoint High-Energy Lasers to prevent collateral damage.*  
> *Against hardened loitering munitions, our 30mm CIWS auto-cannon delivers lethal tungsten airburst clouds.*

#### [02:45 - 03:00] - Part 5: Closing & Impact
> **Presenter:**
